# Project Charter: GLP-1 White-Space Map

**Owner:** Bhanu · **Timeline:** 2 weeks · **Stakeholder (simulated):** GLP-1 brand team (marketing and sales operations)

## Problem statement
Field-force and marketing budgets are limited. The brand needs a data-driven way to find (a) geographies where diabetes/obesity burden is high but GLP-1 use is low, and (b) prescribers with large diabetes panels who rarely prescribe GLP-1s.

## Decisions this supports
- Territory prioritization and call planning
- Targeting for HCP education programs
- Preparing for demand from the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge (obesity coverage from July 2026)

## Questions and KPIs
| # | Question | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where is the biggest county gap? | White-space gap (patients), penetration |
| 2 | How is GLP-1 use growing? | Claims and YoY growth by molecule/brand, share |
| 3 | Which specialties drive uptake? | GLP-1 share of diabetes Rx by specialty |
| 4 | Whom should reps call first? | Count of Untapped HCPs and their diabetes claims |
| 5 | Where will Bridge demand appear? | Obesity prevalence × low current uptake flag |

## In scope / out of scope
- **In:** Medicare Part D 2019–2024, county and prescriber level, diabetes drug classes
- **Out:** commercial/Medicaid claims, patient-level data, pricing/rebates, causal analysis

## Success criteria
- Pipeline reproducible from scratch with one command
- At least 90% of GLP-1 claims mapped to a county
- National GLP-1 totals within 1% of CMS published totals
- Dashboard and one-page memo with 3 clear recommendations
